# Clothing moodboard (October 2026)

A moodboard of reference garments from other brands, shared by the founders as inspiration for the Happy Human clothing line. These are **references, not our designs**. The people in the photos are other brands' models.

![[Attachments/Clothing moodboard 2026-10.png]]

## Patterns across the board
- **Small, quiet logos:** a single small chest logo or tiny wordmark, often tone-on-tone. This matches "minimal print or logo" in [[Brand Strategy]].
- **Simple basics:** tees, long-sleeves and hoodies with relaxed, slightly boxy fits.
- **Muted colours:** black, navy, sage green and powder or heather blue. Calm, wearable colours, not neon.
- **An occasional message piece:** one short, bold line of text ("NIGHT. NIGHT."). This is the format for the "motivating messages to the wearer".
- **Unisex** styling and friendly, natural photography with real-looking people.
