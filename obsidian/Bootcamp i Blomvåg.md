# Bootcamp i Blomvåg

A free, community group-training class in Blomvåg run by trainer [[People/Kaja Haugen|Kaja Haugen]], co-founder of Happy Human. Organised through a Facebook group (94 members as of October 2026). Source: [[Sources/Bootcamp i Blomvåg - Facebook-gruppe]].

## The offer
- **What:** A playful, intense hour of functional training: strength and cardio using bodyweight and simple equipment, circuit training (8–10 stations), and mobility.
- **Who for:** Everyone, from beginners to the very fit. Every exercise is shown with easier and harder alternatives.
- **Kids welcome:** No babysitter? Bring the kids. Sessions are adapted on the spot and sometimes combined with a *leketreff* (playdate) for the children.
- **Length:** about 60 minutes.
- **Price:** free.
- **Where:** the gym hall at Blomvåg Barnehage in winter; outdoors in spring and summer when the weather allows.
- **When:** irregular, announced in the group. Seen so far: weekday evenings 17:30–18:30 (Thursdays) and weekend mornings at 10:00 (Saturdays).

## Session formats seen
- **Classic circuit:** 8–10 stations plus a fun finisher.
- **Two-part hour:** 30 min pulse-focused bodyweight strength, then 30 min dynamic flexibility and mobility.

## How it's run today
- The trainer posts each session in the Facebook group, often as a **poll** where members tick whether they're coming. This gives a rough headcount.
- When there are several possible dates, a poll lets members choose. The two March options split almost evenly (52 % vs 48 %).
- Reminders go out the day before, and the trainer tags `@alle` to reach everyone.
- Members reply in comments to say they can't come or that they're bringing kids.

## The trainer: Kaja Haugen
Full profile: [[People/Kaja Haugen]]

- Certified personal trainer (Active Education) and MSc in Sport Science from the Norwegian School of Sport Sciences (Norges idrettshøgskole).
- Over 7 years as a PT and group instructor in Oslo, with hundreds of clients and about 3,000 PT hours.
- Further training in kettlebell, Soma Move, performance coaching, and training during and after pregnancy.
- Now works at Antidoping Norge.
- **Philosophy:** training should be positive for both body and mind, with a holistic view of health. She sees how thoughts about body, food and training can turn harmful, and wants people to feel mastery (*mestring*) and joy in training (*treningsglede*).

## Key messages and tone
- "Her er alle velkommen" / "Vi har plass til alle": inclusive, for everyone.
- Playful, warm, informal; Norwegian, with occasional dialect from members.
- Low barrier: free, kids allowed, alternatives for every level.
- "Spre budskapet": word of mouth, bring friends.

## Pain points visible in the current setup
These are observations from the group posts, not confirmed needs:
- Sign-up and headcount rely on Facebook polls, which are only approximate ("Jeg kommer kanskje").
- Session info is spread across posts, and members who aren't on Facebook can't see it.
- No way to track how many children are coming, though that affects planning.
- Choosing dates by poll is manual each time.
