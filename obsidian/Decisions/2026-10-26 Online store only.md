# Online store only

## Context
Happy Human will sell its own clothing line (casual wear and athleisure). The founders had to choose between a physical shop, an online store, or both.

## Decision
The clothing line is sold **only through an online store**. There will be no physical shop.

## Alternatives considered
- Physical shop: rejected because of cost.
- Online and physical: rejected for the same reason.

## Consequences
- The website must carry the brand: product pages, photography and checkout matter more.
- Instagram, influencers and ambassadors become the main way people discover the clothes (see [[Brand Strategy]]).
- Meet-ups and group workouts are the only place people meet the brand in person, so they double as the chance to see and try the clothes.
- Still open: whether the store is built into `web/` or runs on a separate e-commerce platform.
