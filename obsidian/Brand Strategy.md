# Brand Strategy

What Happy Human is, who it's for, and how it will reach people, according to the founders' answers in [[Sources/Happy Human brand questionnaire]].

## In one line
**Happy Human is a lifestyle brand for health and happiness:** a clothing line (casual wear and athleisure), fitness services (group classes and personal training) and a blog. All of it is built around the idea that body and mind are connected, and that being a "happy human" is about how you live every day, not only how you train.

## The three parts
| Part | What it is | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------|
| **Clothing line** (second) | Casual wear and athleisure with minimal print or logo. Some pieces carry motivating messages to the wearer. | Sold **online only** through **Shopify**, to keep costs down. Quality, sustainable materials and design are the selling points. Launches after training. |
| **Fitness services** (first) | **Free** group classes and **paid** personal training, with a holistic wellness approach. | Launches first. [[Bootcamp i Blomvåg]] is the existing community class this grows from. Run by [[People/Kaja Haugen\|Kaja]]. |
| **Blog / content** | Mental health, nutrition, workout tips, outdoor tips, success stories, and "what it means to be truly happy on the inside". | Feeds Instagram and the community. |

## Target audience
- **Age:** 18–44.
- **Interests:** mental health, physical health and wellbeing.
- **Mindset:** wants to be true to themselves, be happy, and spread happiness to others.
- Many in the upper part of this range are parents. That fits the bootcamp's "kids welcome" approach and the founder's wish for their own children to grow up healthy.

## What sets us apart (USP)
1. **Exceptional quality.**
2. **Sustainable resources.**
3. **Appealing design.**
4. **A genuine, true brand** that promotes health and happiness for everyone.

The combination is the point: other clothing brands don't run training, and other gyms don't make clothes or share this message about the whole person.

## Fitness philosophy
**Holistic wellness.** The brain and body are connected, and what we eat and do every day matters more than the few workouts we do each week. This matches Kaja's background (sport science, anti-doping, eating disorder support) and the [[Bootcamp i Blomvåg]] values of *mestring* and *treningsglede*.

## Brand look
- **Clothing:** minimal. Little print or logo, so the quality and design speak for themselves. From the [[Sources/Clothing moodboard 2026-10|clothing moodboard]] (Humanrace, Jordan, The North Face, Tufte, Curry Brand, Human Made):
  - Basics: tees, long-sleeves and hoodies with a relaxed, unisex fit.
  - One small chest logo, often tone-on-tone.
  - Muted colours: black, navy, sage green, powder and heather blue.
  - Now and then, a short bold statement as the "motivating message" piece.
- **Logo:** the **H monogram + "Happy Human" wordmark** (confirmed). See [[Decisions/2026-10-26 H monogram as the logo]] and [[Features/Brand Identity]].

## Marketing and community
- **Main channel:** Instagram.
- **Influencer marketing** and **ambassadors** who promote "the Happy Human movement".
- **Community:** Instagram, influencers, meet-ups and group workouts (like the bootcamp).

## Long-term vision
> Make the world a better place by having more happy and true people who want to be nice to each other.

The founder wants their children to grow up healthy, physically and mentally, in a world where people support each other and stay true to themselves.

## What this means for the product (for agents)
- **Web app scope, in order:**
  1. Training: group classes (free; schedule and sign-up with headcount and kids) and personal training (paid; info and booking).
  2. Blog.
  3. Clothing: the store runs on **Shopify**, not in `web/`. `web/` links to it, or later shows products through the Storefront API.
- **Nutrition content** must follow the health-safe rules in `AGENTS.md`: food as energy and wellbeing, never calories, "clean eating" or weight loss.
- **Motivating messages on clothing** count as user-facing copy. They should be positive and inclusive, never about appearance or pressure. Run them past the `health-content-reviewer`.
- **"Sustainable" is a marketing claim.** In Norway, environmental claims in marketing must be accurate and documented, and stricter EU rules (Directive 2024/825) are on their way into Norwegian law. Don't write general claims such as "bærekraftig", "miljøvennlig" or "klimanøytral". Only use specific, documented facts. See [[Research/2026-10-26 Sustainable clothing options]] for allowed wording.
