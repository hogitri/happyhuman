# Happy Human brand questionnaire

Founder answers to a brand and business questionnaire, given to the project on 2026-10-26. The text is kept as written. It doesn't say which founder wrote the answers (the last answer is in first person). For the structured summary, see [[Brand Strategy]].

## Target audience
**Q:** Who is your primary target audience for both the clothing line and the fitness services? What age group, interests, and lifestyle do they typically have?

**A:** Age 18 - 44, interested in mental health, physical health and wellbeing, want to be true to themself and be a happy person and spread happiness to others.

## Unique selling proposition (USP)
**Q:** What sets "Happy Human" apart from other clothing and sportswear brands, as well as fitness centers and personal training services?

**A:** Exceptional quality, sustainable resources, appealing design, genuine and true brand that want to promote health and happiness to all.

## Clothing aesthetic
**Q:** Could you describe the style and vibe of the clothing line? Are you focusing on casual wear, athleisure, or a specific aesthetic that resonates with your brand's message?

**A:** Casual wear, athleisure with minimal print or logo. In some cases we will design clothes with motivating messages to the wearer.

## Fitness philosophy
**Q:** What kind of fitness philosophy or approach will you adopt in your group classes and personal training services to align with the concept of being a "happy human"?

**A:** Holistic wellness, as we know that our brain and body is connected, and what we eat and do in our everyday is more important than the few workouts we do every week.

## Content strategy
**Q:** How do you plan to approach your blogging efforts? What specific topics or themes related to happiness and well-being will you be addressing?

**A:** We will cover mental health, nutrition, workout tips, outdoor tips, success stories and everything related to what it means to be truly happy on the inside.

## Logo and branding
**Q:** Have you finalized your logo and branding elements? If not, what values or emotions do you want your logo and brand identity to convey?

**A:** We have landed on a minimalistic logo icon in addition to the name Happy Human.

## Distribution channels
**Q:** How do you plan to distribute your clothing line? Will you have a physical store, an online store, or a combination of both?

**A:** We will only have an online store to reduce cost.

## Marketing strategy
**Q:** What strategies do you intend to use to promote your brand and attract customers to your clothing line and fitness services?

**A:** We intend to use Instagram as our main channel, in addition to influencer marketing and have ambassadors that can promote the happy human movement.

## Community building
**Q:** How do you envision building a community around the "Happy Human" brand? Will you use social media, events, or other methods to engage with your audience?

**A:** Instagram, influencers, meet ups and group workouts.

## Long-term vision
**Q:** What is your long-term vision for "Happy Human"? How do you see the brand evolving and expanding over the next few years?

**A:** My long-term vision for "Happy Human" is that we can make the world a better place, by rooming more happy and true people that want to be nice to each other. I want my children to grow up healthy, both physically and mentally in a world where people support each other and stay true to themself.
