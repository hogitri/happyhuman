# Group training market: Sotra and Askøy

## Question
Who offers group training (gruppetrening) in Øygarden kommune (Sotra: Straume, Kolltveit, Fjell, Ågotnes and the rest) and Askøy kommune (Kleppestø, Strusshamn, Florvåg)? What do they run, what does it cost, how is it booked, and where are the gaps that matter for Happy Human and [[Bootcamp i Blomvåg]]?

## Summary
- **The market is dominated by gym chains with 24/7 access and app booking.** On Sotra, **MOVA** runs five centres (Straume, Straumeparken, Ågotnes, Ågotnes Torg, Skogsvåg), three of them bought from Sprek og Blid in 2023. Group classes are concentrated at the staffed centres in **Straume** and **Ågotnes Torg**. **NEXT** (24/7, no binding period) has arrived in Straume and runs two Askøy centres. On Askøy, **Sporty** (formerly Family Sports Club) is in Kleppestø, NEXT is in Strusshamn and Florvåg, and **Storebotn Gym / Botn Boxen** runs functional, HYROX and bootcamp classes.
- **No SATS, EVO, Fresh Fitness or 3T on Sotra or Askøy** was found. The nearest SATS is SATS Vestkanten in Loddefjord (Bergen), just over the Sotra bridge.
- **The independent and community layer is thin.** One yoga studio on Askøy (YouMe Yoga), a small community yoga group on Sotra (PurYogaSotra), a club-run gym (Sotra Sportssenter, owned by Sotra Sportsklubb), the municipal Friskliv offers (by referral), and seniortrim. **No CrossFit box, reformer pilates studio or parent/postnatal training (mammatrening, trilletrim) was found** in either municipality.
- **Typical prices (confirmed):** chain memberships cost about **399–799 kr/month**, mostly with 12-month binding. Sotra Sportssenter starts at 150 kr/month for club members. SATS Vestkanten drop-in is 200 kr/day. Free, low-barrier group training like the Blomvåg bootcamp is almost absent in the commercial market.
- **Demand side:** Øygarden has about 40,800 residents (Q2 2026), grew 1.34 % in 2025 (its fastest since 2020), and about 22 % are children. Askøy has about 30,700. About 9,700 people commute out of Øygarden and about 9,400 out of Askøy. The new Sotrasambandet (Sotra bridge link) is delayed and **will not open in 2027**; no new date has been set.
- **Main opportunity (our judgement):** low-cost, inclusive, family-friendly group training close to where people live, especially outside Straume (north Sotra, Kolltveit, the south and west of the island, Askøy outside Kleppestø) and at times that suit parents. That is where the chains are weakest and where the Blomvåg bootcamp already works.

## Findings

### 1. Gym chains

**MOVA (Øygarden and Askøy).** MOVA Group was formed in autumn 2022 by merging 14 gym brands into one identity ([t-i.no](https://t-i.no/mova-fra-14-til-en-merkevare-for-sommeren/)). In June 2023 it bought Sprek Invest AS, which owned three Sprek og Blid centres on Sotra (Skogsvåg, Ågotnes and Straume) with more than 2,000 members combined ([t-i.no, 21.06.2023](https://t-i.no/mova-group-styrker-sin-posisjon-kjoper-sprek-og-blid-sentre-pa-sotra/)). Older listings (for example on FINN) still say "Sprek & Blid".

- **Class concepts (chain-wide):** Kropp og Sjel (yoga, pilates, mobility), studio classes, **Sprek Voksen** (slower tempo, lower music, aimed at older adults, with a social focus), running classes on treadmills, cycling, step and coordination, strength, and hall cardio. New concepts include Performance, Power Pilates and MOVA Sterk ([mova.no/gruppetimer](https://www.mova.no/gruppetimer)). Which of these run on Sotra, and how many per week, **could not be verified**, because the timetable is loaded by script.
- **Prices (campaign prices to 31.12.2026, 12-month binding):** Lokalt from **549 kr** (Straume, Skogsvåg) or **399 kr** (Kleppestø); Fleks **699 kr** (all centres in the region, classes except premium); Premium **799 kr** (all of Norway, including premium classes). Without binding it costs 100 kr/month more, plus a 250 kr joining fee and 299 kr/year admin fee ([mova.no/priser](https://www.mova.no/priser); centre pages above). The centre pages show prices with a footnote marker ("5491", "3991"), which we read as 549 and 399. Drop-in is not offered; there is a 7-day trial instead.
- **Booking:** the MOVA app for door entry, plus MOVA's own online booking for classes.

**NEXT Fitness.** A newer 24/7 chain with a fixed low price and no binding period. Its Bergen-area centres include **Straume** (Smålonane 4, about 400 m², reported by t-i.no on 01.08.2025), **Askøy (Strusshamn senter)** and **Florvåg** ([nextfitness.no](https://nextfitness.no/), [t-i.no](https://t-i.no/next-apner-pa-straume/)).
- NEXT Askøy runs instructor-led **yoga, spinning, pilates, strength, cardio, step and zumba**, booked through **iBooking** (nextaskoy.ibooking.no) ([nextfitness.no/next-askoy](https://nextfitness.no/next-askoy/)).
- The NEXT Straume page does not mention group classes ([nextfitness.no/next-straume](https://nextfitness.no/next-straume/)).
- NEXT centres are also bookable through the aggregator **Bruce Studios** ([Bruce: NEXT Straume](https://www.brucestudios.com/studio/next-straume-10409)). Prices are not published on the NEXT pages.

**Sporty (formerly Family Sports Club), Kleppestø.** Holmedalen 6B, 5300 Kleppestø. The page links to group class booking and a timetable but the classes are not listed; it uses the Sporty app; age limit 15; current campaign "Tren gratis til 2027 – ingen binding" ([sporty.no](https://sporty.no/treningssenter/sporty-kleppesto)). In June 2025 Family Sports Club, Aktiv Trening and Aktiv365 merged under the Sporty brand, giving about 90 clubs ([Leisure Opportunities](https://www.leisureopportunities.co.uk/news/Sporty-AS-launches-as-a-new-brand-for-the-Norwegian-fitness-market-expands-its-coverage-in-Norway/356044)).

**SATS, EVO, Fresh Fitness, 3T.** No centres were found on Sotra or Askøy. SATS lists Bergen centres but none west of Loddefjord. **SATS Vestkanten** (Loddefjordveien 2, Bergen) is the nearest premium option for Sotra commuters: 13 class categories including Senior, Pilates, Yoga, Dance and HIIT; childcare (barnepass) listed; Basic 749 kr, Flexible 799 kr, Premium 999 kr/month; **drop-in 200 kr/day** ([sats.no Vestkanten](https://www.sats.no/treningssenter/vestkanten)). SATS has acquired and rebranded Fresh Fitness in Norway (search summary, not verified at the primary source). EVO and 3T: nothing found in the area (absence of evidence, not proof).

### 3. Municipal, senior and voluntary offers

- **Øygarden kommune, Friskliv og meistring** runs: Gågruppe (walking group), Livsstil kondisjon, Livsstil styrke, Styrke intro, MediYoga, and a yoga course. In cooperation with others: Aktiv mot kreft, AKTIV+, and a COPD course. It also recommends **Seniortrim i Hjeltefjorden Arena**, **Trim for seniorar – Skogsskiftet**, a Monday turgruppe, golf for all, and pool training ([oygarden.kommune.no](https://www.oygarden.kommune.no/tjenester/helse-omsorg-og-velferd/friskliv-og-meistring/treningstilbod/)). - **DNT:** Sotra Øygarden Turlag (founded 2020) ([ut.no](https://ut.no/gruppe/15517483/sotra-ygarden-turlag)). Askøy Turlag runs a weekly "Onsdagsturen" for adults ([annual report 2025](https://www.dnt.no/contentassets/50e32118b98b463299dc94b8479feb8e/arsrapport-askoy-turlag-2025.pdf)).

## Gaps and opportunities
These are our own judgements based on the findings above, not source claims.

1. **Geography.** Staffed group training on Sotra clusters in **Straume** (MOVA, Sotra Sportssenter, In Shape, NEXT) and **Ågotnes Torg**. North Øygarden (where Blomvåg is), Kolltveit, the south and west of Sotra, and Askøy outside Kleppestø and Strusshamn rely on self-service gyms or have nothing. With bridge queues and a long commute, local sessions close to home have value.
2. **Parents and families.** Only Botn Boxen (weekend childcare) and SATS Vestkanten (barnepass) mention childcare. No mammatrening, trilletrim or kid-friendly sessions were found. Øygarden has an above-average share of children. **This is the clearest gap, and it is exactly what the Blomvåg bootcamp offers.**
3. **Price and barrier.** Chains need 12-month binding at 549–799 kr. Low-cost options are mainly the club gym (from 150 kr) and the referral-based Friskliv offers. There is room for free or very cheap drop-in community sessions with no binding.
4. **Outdoor training.** No regular outdoor bootcamp or fellestrening (shared community training) group was found on Sotra. DNT covers hiking, not training.
5. **Beginners and inclusive tone.** Several providers say "for all levels", but the chain marketing is generic. A strong *mestring* and *treningsglede* angle with alternatives for every exercise is still distinctive.
6. **Seniors.** Seniors are partly served: MOVA Sprek Voksen, seniortrim, Friskliv, Sotra Sportssenter's 60+ membership, Storebotn 60+ price. This is not the top gap, though "family across generations" could be.
7. **Times.** Timetables were not readable, so time-of-day gaps **could not be assessed**. Commuters leave early and return late because of bridge queues, so weekend mornings and early evenings are a reasonable guess to test, not a finding.
8. **Tools.** Community trainers use Facebook. No local tool serves free or low-cost community sessions with RSVP, headcount (including children) and reminders. This matches the pain points in [[Bootcamp i Blomvåg]].

## Options compared
Ways Happy Human could position itself in this market:

| Option | Pros | Cons | Fit for Happy Human |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Compete with gyms (studio and membership) | Proven revenue model | Capital-heavy; MOVA, NEXT and Sporty are entrenched with 24/7 access and low prices | Low |
| B. Community, family-friendly group sessions (outdoor or in rented halls), across many neighbourhoods | Fills the clearest gaps (location, families, price, outdoor); builds on Blomvåg | Hard to make money from free sessions; relies on trainers | High |
| C. Tool for community trainers (sign-up, headcount, kids, reminders) to replace Facebook | Real pain seen in Blomvåg; local providers all use Facebook; scales beyond Sotra | Must beat "free and already in Facebook"; small local market alone | High (as product), especially combined with B |
| D. Partner with municipalities and clubs (Friskliv, idrettslag, barnehager and schools as venues) | Cheap venues, credibility, access to target groups | Slow public processes | Medium, as a channel |

## Recommendation
Our judgement: Happy Human should not compete with the gym chains. The local evidence supports **B + C**: low-barrier, family-friendly group training held close to home in areas outside Straume, run with a simple tool that replaces the Facebook polls. Blomvåg is the pilot. A natural next test is a second location in another underserved area (for example Kolltveit, south or west Sotra, or Askøy outside Kleppestø), with weekend mornings and early evenings and "barn velkommen" as the main message. Use Friskliv and idrettslag as partners and venue sources (option D). This should be recorded as a decision only once the founders agree on the product direction.
