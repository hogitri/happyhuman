# Skogsvåg IL: «Støtt Kaja til futsal-EM»

Blog post on Skogsvåg IL's site, dated 29 November 2017, shared by Hoang on 2026-11-03.

Summary (not a full copy):
- Former Skogsvåg IL player **Kaja Haugen** is on the Norwegian women's futsal national team going to the **European Championship (EM) in Spain**.
- It is described as historic: the first time Norway takes part in a futsal EM for women ("første gang Norge stiller til EM i futsal for damer").
- The post asks for support through a crowdfunding project for the team's trip.
