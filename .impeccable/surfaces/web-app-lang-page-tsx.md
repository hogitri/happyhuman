# Surface brief: happyhuman.no front page

Scope: the brand front page at `/no` and `/en` (Persuade). The bootcamp page that used to live here moves to `/[lang]/trening`.

Audience and job: people 18–44 around Øygarden/Bergen deciding whether Happy Human is "for someone like me". Action: join a free class (today via the Facebook group). Proof on hand: the two founders and their real backgrounds, the bootcamp facts, photos of the place. No class photos, testimonials or prices exist; never invent them.

Visual direction: founder portrait editorial, styled after The Pilates Class (user-pinned reference, 2026-11-03: "personal, professional, clean, modern").

Memorable moment: the founders' letter, set large, signed by Kaja and Hoang, right after a calm full-bleed fjord hero.

Unresolved: real training photos; better portrait of Hoang (427px source); Instagram handle; PT prices; clothing and blog launch dates.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The movement is two real people inviting you in. The page refuses the gym-ad default (dark, loud, bodies) and the template card grid; it reads like a calm, well-edited founders' profile where the invitation is personal.

OWN-WORLD: Warm paper white and sand bands, one deep coastal teal band, charcoal ink. A regular-weight geometric sans for headings and body, a light serif for leads, quotes and the letter. Square corners everywhere: photos, buttons, fields. One pale sun-yellow fill for the single main action. Full-bleed photography, thin hairlines, generous whitespace. The H monogram + wordmark centred in the header.

STORY: See the coast and one sentence (what this is) → read the founders' letter (why) → meet Kaja and Hoang (who) → see what the movement offers now and next (training free, PT, clothing and blog coming) → believe there is room for you → join a free class.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header 72px: three links left, centred logo, square pale-sun "Bli med på en gratis time" right. Below, a full-bleed photo of Hjeltefjorden from Rong filling the rest of the viewport. In the pale sky on the left: h1 in the sans at ~72px regular, a serif lead under it, the primary button. Bottom-left on the photo: a small founder lockup, two square portraits with "Startet av Kaja og Hoang".

FORM: Grounded candidate 1 of my ordered list (founder portrait editorial), fused with the user-pinned The Pilates Class style; chosen as IMPECCABLE’S PICK in re-roll round 2. seed:0629473ce5bca898. Signature interaction: the offer section's tab switch (Fellestimer / Personlig trening / Klær / Blogg), one large photo and text per tab, items not yet launched clearly marked as coming. Motion grammar: slow image fade and a 12px rise on the letter as it enters, nothing else.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
