# Launch training first, clothing second

## Context
Happy Human has three parts: fitness services, a clothing line and a blog ([[Brand Strategy]]). The founders had to choose what to launch first.

## Decision
**Training launches first, and the clothing line comes second.** The fitness services (free group classes and paid personal training) are the starting point. The clothing line and online store follow later.

## Alternatives considered
- Clothing first: needs stock, production and money up front, before there's an audience.
- Both at once: splits a small founding team's time and money.

## Consequences
- Training is the start because it already exists: [[Bootcamp i Blomvåg]] has a community (94 Facebook group members), and Kaja is an experienced trainer.
- The community built through training (group workouts, meet-ups, Instagram) becomes the audience for the clothing launch.
- **Web app priority:** pages for group classes (schedule and sign-up) and personal training (info and booking) come first. The store comes later, on Shopify ([[Decisions/2026-10-26 Shopify for the online store]]).
- The blog can start early, since it costs little and builds the audience for both.
- Clothing can appear early in a small way, e.g. trainers and ambassadors wearing branded tees, without opening the store.
