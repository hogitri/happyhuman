# Hoang "Hogi" Minh Trinh

Co-founder of Happy Human. A serial founder and startup investor with a PT background, based in Bergen.

![[Hoang Minh Trinh - portrait.jpg|240]]

*Portrait in a Centrum Tigers basketball jersey (#22), shared by Hoang in October 2026.*

Source: [[Sources/Hoang Minh Trinh - LinkedIn]]

## In short
- **Now:** Head of Ventures at Agens (since Nov 2022) and Founding Partner at Atlas Accelerator (since Apr 2024), running an accelerator and investing in early-stage tech startups.
- **Founder experience:** co-founded Firi (2018–2021), first as business developer and then as CMO.
- **Boards:** chair of Haralt; board member at Proppy, EzBench, Nexus Connect and Kiip.app.
- **Education:** Bachelor's in Creativity, Innovation and Business Development (Kristiania), plus a Personal Trainer qualification from NIH and Performance Specialist certification (Scandinavian Top Team).
- **Languages:** English, Norwegian, Vietnamese.

## Role in Happy Human
- Chair of the board of **HAPPY HUMAN AS** (org.nr. 933 037 495, founded 2024-01-31). See [[Project Overview#Company]].

## What Hoang brings to Happy Human
- **Building startups:** has co-founded several companies and supported thousands of entrepreneurs over a decade.
- **Business and growth:** business development, sales, marketing strategy, growth, SEO and market research.
- **Funding and network:** venture capital and an accelerator (Atlas, with DNB as a partner), board roles across several startups, and a wide network (3,600+ followers).
- **Training background:** PT qualification from NIH, a Performance Specialist certification and strength training. This overlaps with [[People/Kaja Haugen|Kaja]]'s field.
- **Mentoring:** mentor and jury for Ungt Entreprenørskap, GründerAcademy, Hack The Crisis and Emax.
- **Sharing economy:** works to promote greener consumption, for example as chair of Haralt.
