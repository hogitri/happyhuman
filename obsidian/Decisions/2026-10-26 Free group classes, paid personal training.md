# Free group classes, paid personal training

## Context
[[Bootcamp i Blomvåg]] is free today. A design for a 10-session punch card (*klippekort*) suggested group classes might become paid ([[Features/Brand Identity#Punch card (klippekort)]]). The founders needed to decide how training is paid for.

## Decision
- **Group classes are free.**
- **Personal training (PT) is paid.**

## Alternatives considered
- Paid group classes (e.g. with a punch card): would bring in income, but would raise the barrier and go against the bootcamp's "Vi har plass til alle".
- Everything free: no income from training.

## Consequences
- Free group classes are the way into the community and the brand. They keep the barrier low (see the Product principles in `AGENTS.md`), and the paid PT and later the clothing line are where the money comes from.
- **Group class sign-up** only needs a headcount (and "bringing kids?"), not payment. This replaces the Facebook polls.
- **PT booking** needs prices, packages and a payment method. Prices aren't set yet. In Norway, Vipps is the expected way to pay.
- The punch card design no longer fits free group classes. It could be reused for PT packages (e.g. 10 PT sessions) if wanted.
- The landing page and copy should say clearly that group classes are free.
