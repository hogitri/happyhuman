# H monogram as the logo

## Context
There were two competing logo directions ([[Features/Brand Identity]]):
- **Blocky wordmark:** all-caps, square, chamfered display letters (`HAPPY HUMAN`, with an `HA HU` patch).
- **H monogram:** a capital H made of two bars joined by two arches bowing towards each other, plus "Happy Human" in a plain sans. It's on the back of the bootcamp punch card.

The founders said they had "landed on a minimalistic logo icon in addition to the name Happy Human" ([[Brand Strategy]]), and confirmed that this means the H monogram.

## Decision
**The H monogram, with the "Happy Human" wordmark, is the Happy Human logo.** The blocky wordmark is no longer the main logo.

## Alternatives considered
- Blocky wordmark: strong and sporty, but wide and loud. It fits the minimal clothing aesthetic and holistic wellbeing tone less well.

## Consequences
- **Clothing:** the H icon works as the small chest logo or embroidery the [[Sources/Clothing moodboard 2026-10|clothing moodboard]] calls for.
- **Web:** use the H monogram for the site header, favicon and social avatar.
- **Missing:** a clean vector file (SVG) of the H monogram and the name of the wordmark typeface. Today we only have it as an image from the punch card. Ask the designer.
- The blocky wordmark mockups stay in the vault as history. Use the blocky "HAPPY <name>" variant only if the founders want it for something specific.
