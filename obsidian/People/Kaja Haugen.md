# Kaja Haugen

Co-founder of Happy Human. A trainer and sport scientist working in anti-doping and health prevention, based in the Bergen region.

Sources: [[Sources/Kaja Haugen - LinkedIn]], [[Sources/Bootcamp i Blomvåg - Facebook-gruppe]]

## In short
- **Now:** County Coordinator for Vestland at Antidoping Norge (since Sep 2022), based in Bergen.
- **Education:** MSc in Sport Science, Norwegian School of Sport Sciences (NIH), plus PT certification from Active Education.
- **Training experience:** about 7 years as a PT and group fitness instructor at SiO in Oslo (2014–2021), with hundreds of clients and around 3,000 PT hours.
- **Community:** runs [[Bootcamp i Blomvåg]], a free group-training class with 94 Facebook group members.

## What she brings to Happy Human
- **Exercise expertise:** hands-on PT and group-training experience, with further training in kettlebell, Soma Move, performance coaching, and training during and after pregnancy.
- **Health and prevention:** years of work on doping, body image and healthy training, including a prevention programme for young inmates in Vestland (with Bjørgvin prison and Kriminalomsorgen) on youth, steroids and masculinity.
- **Eating disorders and body image:** volunteer advisor at Rådgivning om spiseforstyrrelser. This ties in with her stated concern about how thoughts on body, food and training can turn harmful.
- **Research:** a sport science master's, plus research assistant and research coordinator roles.
- **Marketing and quality:** quality and marketing coordinator at SiO.
- **Community building:** she started and runs a local bootcamp community from scratch.

## Values and philosophy
From her bootcamp description:
- Training should be positive for **both body and mind**, with a **holistic view of health**.
- The goal is for people to feel **mestring** (mastery) and **treningsglede** (joy in training).
- Inclusive: "Vi har plass til alle." Every exercise has alternatives for every level, and kids are welcome.
